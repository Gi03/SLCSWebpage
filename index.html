History

Established in 1935, formerly known as Saint Louis School. Now Saint Louis College of Solano.

Founder:
Rev. Fr. Jose Waffelaert, CICM
1934
Opened on June 10, 1935

First College President:
Rev. Fr. Victor M. Abijay, Ph.D.
2024

Mission

Guided by the Gospel values, DBES commits to quality and transformative Catholic Education for the growth and development of stakeholders through contextualized inclusive learner-centered curriculum, enhanced employees' professional developmental opportunities, and strengthened partnership with the pursuit of Christian discipleship and witnessing, community building, and stewardship for the service of God, family, and country.

Vision

A vibrant Catholic by the Gospel values, DBES commits to quality and transformative Catholic Education for the growth and development of stakeholders through contextualized inclusive learner-centered curriculum, enhanced employees' professional opportunities, and strengthened partnership with the stakeholders in the pursuit of Christian discipleship and witnessing, community building, stewardship for the service of God, family, and country.

Diocese of Bayombong Educational System

VISION

We, the Diocese of Bayombong Educational System, envision a witnessing and collaborative community of Catholic Schools with stakeholders of deep spirituality, diversified competence and strengthened service orientation.

MISSION

We commit ourselves to provide quality Catholic Education and formation for the growth and development of students/pupils in our schools and to become Christian disciples in this ever-changing world.
