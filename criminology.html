About the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Criminology is a four-year degree program that prepares students for careers in law enforcement, crime prevention, criminal investigation, corrections and public safety, grounded in Catholic values and service to the community.

What You Will Study

  • Law enforcement administration and police operations
  • Criminal law, evidence and criminal procedure
  • Criminalistics and forensic science
  • Crime detection, investigation and correctional administration
  • Criminological theories, ethics and human behavior

Career Opportunities

  • Police and other law enforcement agencies
  • Jail, corrections and parole services
  • Fire protection and public safety
  • Criminal investigation and security management
  • Further studies in law and criminology
